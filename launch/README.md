# Launch reel

`muffin-muse-launch.mp4` is a 16-second vertical video (1080×1920, 30 fps) with an original synthesised soundtrack, made for Instagram Reels and Stories.

* `music.py` generates the soundtrack (`music.wav`).
* `render.py` renders the video. It expects the portrait photos in `img/` and needs Pillow, NumPy and imageio-ffmpeg.

The cake photos are Unsplash stock images. Re-render with Diksha's own cakes before using the reel as a portfolio.
