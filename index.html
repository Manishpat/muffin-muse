Meet the muse

A little kitchen, a lot of heart.

Muffin Muse began with a simple belief — that a cake should feel as personal as the moment it celebrates.

Hi, I'm Diksha. Every Muffin Muse creation is baked from scratch, in small batches, only after you order. I sketch each design around your story — the colours you love, the flavour that makes you smile, the little detail only your family will notice. No shortcuts, no shelf-cakes, just honest ingredients and a lot of patience with a piping bag.

Whether it's a first birthday, a fiftieth anniversary or a Tuesday that needed something sweet, I'd love to bake for you.

How to order

From idea to first slice in four easy steps.

  1. 01

    Tell us the occasion

    Pick the celebration, who it's for and any milestone. Takes seconds.

  2. 02

    Design your cake

    Choose size, flavour, egg or eggless, theme and colours. Upload a reference photo if you have one.

  3. 03

    Pick date & delivery

    Choose your date, a time slot and home delivery or self-pickup.

  4. 04

    Diksha confirms

    We personally reach out to confirm design, price and availability. No login, no account — ever.

Please book at least 1 day in advance. For wedding and multi-tier cakes, a week's notice is ideal.
